## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on nutritional bioinformatics and human metabolism, with emphasis on the classification of nutrients, nutritional composition of foods, dietary supplements, metabolic pathways, and the biological importance of nutrients in human health.

The project combines biological research with basic computational data analysis. Nutritional information for more than 30 selected foods was organized into a structured Excel database. The database was used to examine calories and major macronutrients, including protein, carbohydrates, and fat.

Scientific diagrams and visualizations were prepared using BioRender to explain nutrient functions and metabolic concepts. The complete work was documented through a scientific report, technical documentation, and presentation.
